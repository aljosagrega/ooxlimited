Animation and 3D modeling 

We provide animation and 3D modeling services for mobile games, interactive products, and real-time applications. In mobile production, visual assets need to look strong while staying optimized for performance.

Our approach to 3D modeling for games focuses on clarity, clean silhouettes, and scalable asset pipelines. We build Unity-ready models and animations that run smoothly across mobile devices, while keeping visual quality consistent.

Types of Animation and 3D modeling We Offer

3D Character Modeling

We create optimized 3D character assets built for mobile performance and clean animation workflows. We deliver characters engine-ready, with proper topology and efficient UV layouts for smooth real-time use.

Mobile Game Animation

Frequently Asked Questions

What are animation and 3D modeling services for games?

Animation and 3D modeling services cover the creation of characters, environments, props, and motion systems for mobile and real-time games. We build and optimize assets to maintain performance, visual clarity, and consistent style.

Why is optimization important in mobile 3D modeling?

Mobile game development demands efficient asset creation, as poorly optimized 3D models impact frame rate and memory usage. Our 3D modeling for games balances visual quality and performance from the start.

Do you create both stylized and realistic 3D assets?

Our animation and 3D modeling services adapt to each project’s art direction. We create stylized and semi-realistic assets while maintaining performance based on production goals.

Are your animations Unity-ready?

Our game animation services are built for Unity integration. We deliver assets with proper rigging, animation states, and structure, ready for seamless engine implementation.

Can 3D assets be created during prototyping?

We often use simplified 3D models and animations during early validation phases. Once gameplay is confirmed, we refine assets for full production quality.

Do you work alongside developers during integration?

Yes. Our team works closely with developers to ensure Unity 3D assets and animations integrate seamlessly into gameplay systems.

How do you ensure scalability in art production?

We use structured asset pipelines, consistent naming conventions, and modular modeling approaches to keep projects scalable without compromising visual consistency or performance.

Do you support long-term production?

Yes. Many teams start with a small set of assets and scale into full animation and 3D modeling production as development progresses.
