Frequently Asked Questions

What is rapid prototyping?

Rapid prototyping is the process of building a functional, simplified version of a product to test core functionality, usability, and technical feasibility before full development.

How long does rapid prototyping take?

Rapid prototype development typically takes between 2 to 6 weeks, depending on scope and complexity. The goal is to test the most critical assumptions as quickly as possible.

What is included in rapid prototyping services?

Rapid prototyping services include core mechanics, interactive systems, basic UI, and essential technical architecture needed for meaningful testing. The goal is insight, not polish.

Is rapid prototyping only for games?

No. While rapid prototyping is widely used in game development, it is equally valuable for mobile apps, interactive products, and gamified platforms.

Can a rapid prototype be used for pitching investors?

Yes. A functional rapid prototype is one of the strongest tools for pitching investors or publishers because it demonstrates real interaction rather than just concepts.

What tools do you use for rapid prototyping?

We use Unity and Unreal Engine for rapid prototype development, depending on the technical requirements and target platforms.

Why is rapid prototyping important before full-cycle development?

Rapid prototyping reduces development risk, prevents unnecessary spending, and provides early validation before investing in complete production.
