Game Prototyping Services

Rapid prototyping helps teams validate ideas before committing to full production. Instead of building everything upfront, we create functional prototypes that test core mechanics, UX, and technical feasibility. Our rapid prototyping services focus on building playable, interactive builds fast. 

Rapid prototyping helps teams validate ideas before committing to full production. Instead of building everything upfront, we create functional prototypes that test core mechanics, UX, and technical feasibility.

Our rapid prototyping services focus on building playable, interactive builds fast. This replaces assumptions with measurable insights, reduces risk, and helps teams make confident product decisions early.

Types of Rapid Prototyping We Offer

Unity Rapid Prototyping

We build fast, functional Unity prototypes focused on testing core gameplay mechanics. The goal is a working build you can actually play and evaluate, not a polished product — just enough to know if the idea is worth pursuing.

Game Prototype Development​

For studios and startups building games, we prototype core gameplay loops, monetization systems, and retention mechanics early. You get real answers about what works before committing to full production.

3D Interactive Prototyping

We create interactive 3D prototypes that let you test spatial mechanics, player movement, and environment interactions in a real engine environment. Ideal for validating concepts that are hard to communicate on paper.

Mobile App Prototyping

We build functional mobile app prototypes for iOS and Android that test UX flows, core features, and technical feasibility. Fast to build, easy to test, and ready to share with stakeholders or investors.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is rapid prototyping?

Rapid prototyping is the process of building a functional, simplified version of a product to test core functionality, usability, and technical feasibility before full development begins.

How long does rapid prototyping take?

Typically, rapid prototype development takes between 2 to 6 weeks, depending on scope and complexity. During that time, teams focus on testing the most critical assumptions as early as possible.

 

What is included in rapid prototyping services?

In practice, rapid prototyping services include core mechanics, interactive systems, basic UI, and essential technical architecture needed for meaningful testing. At this stage, the goal is to gain clear insight rather than deliver a polished product.

Is rapid prototyping only for games?

Although teams widely use rapid prototyping in game development, it also applies to mobile apps, interactive products, and gamified platforms. As a result, teams across different industries use this approach to validate ideas before moving into full production.

Can a rapid prototype be used for pitching investors?

Yes. A functional rapid prototype serves as one of the strongest tools for pitching investors or publishers because it demonstrates real interaction instead of abstract concepts.

What tools do you use for rapid prototyping?

We use Unity and Unreal Engine for rapid prototype development, depending on technical requirements and target platforms. This way, we can match the tools to the needs of each project and move faster during early stages.

Why is rapid prototyping important before full-cycle development?

At the same time, rapid prototyping reduces development risk, prevents unnecessary spending, and provides early validation before teams invest in full production.
